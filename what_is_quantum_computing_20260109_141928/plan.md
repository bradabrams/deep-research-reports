# Research Plan: What is Quantum Computing?

## Topic Interpretation & Assumptions

### Core Interpretation
This research will provide a comprehensive exploration of quantum computing—a revolutionary computing paradigm that leverages quantum mechanical phenomena to process information in fundamentally different ways than classical computers.

### Key Assumptions
1. **Audience Level**: General technical audience with some familiarity with computing concepts, but not necessarily physics experts
2. **Scope**: Both theoretical foundations and practical/commercial applications
3. **Timeframe Focus**: Current state (2024-2026) with historical context and future outlook
4. **Geographic Scope**: Global perspective, covering developments in US, China, EU, and other key players
5. **Depth**: Deep enough to explain quantum advantage, but accessible to non-specialists

### Boundaries
- Will cover: Hardware approaches, software/algorithms, applications, market dynamics, key players
- Will not cover: Deep mathematical proofs, detailed physics derivations, classified/military applications

## Research Questions

### Technical/Factual Core (Foundational)

1. **What are the fundamental principles of quantum mechanics that enable quantum computing?**
   - Superposition, entanglement, quantum interference
   - How these differ from classical bit operations

2. **What is a qubit and how does it differ from a classical bit?**
   - Physical implementations (superconducting, trapped ion, photonic, topological)
   - Coherence times and error rates

3. **What are the major quantum computing hardware architectures and their trade-offs?**
   - Superconducting qubits (IBM, Google)
   - Trapped ions (IonQ, Quantinuum)
   - Photonic systems (Xanadu, PsiQuantum)
   - Neutral atoms (QuEra, Pasqal)
   - Topological approaches (Microsoft)

4. **What is quantum error correction and why is it critical?**
   - Types of quantum errors (decoherence, gate errors)
   - Error correction codes and logical qubits
   - The path to fault-tolerant quantum computing

5. **What are the most important quantum algorithms and what advantages do they provide?**
   - Shor's algorithm (cryptography implications)
   - Grover's algorithm (search speedup)
   - Variational quantum algorithms (QAOA, VQE)
   - Quantum simulation algorithms

### Economic/Business Implications

6. **What is the current market size and projected growth of the quantum computing industry?**
   - Hardware, software, and services segments
   - Investment trends (VC, government, corporate R&D)

7. **Which companies are leading quantum computing development and what are their strategies?**
   - Tech giants (IBM, Google, Microsoft, Amazon)
   - Pure-play quantum companies (IonQ, Rigetti, D-Wave)
   - International players (Alibaba, Baidu, European startups)

8. **What industries are most likely to benefit from quantum computing first?**
   - Finance (portfolio optimization, risk analysis)
   - Pharmaceuticals (drug discovery, molecular simulation)
   - Logistics (optimization problems)
   - Cryptography and cybersecurity

9. **What is the "quantum threat" to current cryptographic systems and how are organizations preparing?**
   - Timeline for cryptographically relevant quantum computers
   - Post-quantum cryptography standards (NIST)
   - Migration strategies

### Historical Context

10. **What is the historical development of quantum computing from theoretical concept to physical reality?**
    - Feynman's 1982 proposal
    - Key milestones (first qubit, first algorithms, quantum supremacy claims)
    - Evolution of different hardware approaches

11. **What were the key scientific breakthroughs that made practical quantum computing possible?**
    - Theoretical foundations (Deutsch, Shor, Grover)
    - Engineering achievements (increasing coherence, error correction)

### Critical Perspectives & Challenges

12. **What are the major technical obstacles to building useful quantum computers?**
    - Decoherence and noise
    - Scaling challenges
    - The "NISQ era" limitations

13. **Is "quantum supremacy/advantage" meaningful and has it been achieved?**
    - Google's 2019 claim and IBM's response
    - Recent demonstrations and their significance
    - Debate over practical vs. theoretical advantage

14. **What are the realistic timelines for quantum computers to solve practical problems?**
    - Expert predictions and their track record
    - Milestones needed (number of logical qubits, error rates)

15. **What are the geopolitical implications of quantum computing development?**
    - US-China quantum race
    - National quantum initiatives and funding
    - Export controls and technology restrictions

### Emerging & Future Considerations

16. **How does quantum computing relate to artificial intelligence and machine learning?**
    - Quantum machine learning algorithms
    - Potential synergies and limitations
    - Hybrid classical-quantum approaches

17. **What is quantum networking and the quantum internet?**
    - Quantum key distribution (QKD)
    - Distributed quantum computing
    - Current state of quantum networks

18. **How accessible is quantum computing today for researchers and developers?**
    - Cloud quantum computing platforms
    - Programming frameworks (Qiskit, Cirq, Q#)
    - Educational resources and skills gap

## Research Methodology

### Source Strategy
1. **Primary Technical Sources**: arXiv preprints, Nature, Science, Physical Review journals
2. **Industry Analysis**: McKinsey, BCG, Gartner reports on quantum computing
3. **Company Sources**: IBM, Google, IonQ research blogs and documentation
4. **News & Current Events**: MIT Technology Review, Wired, Ars Technica
5. **Government Reports**: NIST, NSF, DOE quantum initiatives

### Confidence Level Framework
- **HIGH**: 3+ corroborating sources from reputable outlets
- **MEDIUM**: 2 sources or expert consensus with some debate
- **LOW**: Single source or emerging/speculative information
- **DISPUTED**: Active disagreement among credible sources
