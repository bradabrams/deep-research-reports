# Research Plan: AI Data Center Power 2025

## Topic Interpretation & Assumptions

### Core Topic
The intersection of artificial intelligence workloads and data center power consumption, infrastructure, and sustainability challenges in 2025.

### Key Assumptions
1. **Scope**: Focus on power consumption, energy infrastructure, and sustainability challenges specifically driven by AI/ML workloads (training and inference)
2. **Timeframe**: Current state (2025) with historical context from 2020-2024 and projections through 2030
3. **Geographic Coverage**: Global perspective with emphasis on major markets (US, EU, China, emerging markets)
4. **Technology Focus**: GPU/TPU clusters, cooling systems, power delivery, grid integration, and renewable energy
5. **Stakeholders**: Hyperscalers (Microsoft, Google, Amazon, Meta), AI companies (OpenAI, Anthropic), utilities, regulators, environmental groups
6. **Definition of "AI Data Center"**: Facilities primarily or significantly dedicated to AI training and inference workloads, distinct from traditional cloud/enterprise data centers

### Scope Boundaries
- **In Scope**: Power consumption, energy sources, grid impacts, cooling, efficiency metrics, policy/regulation, environmental impact
- **Out of Scope**: General data center operations unrelated to power, detailed chip architecture, AI model performance metrics

## Research Questions

### Technical/Factual Core (5 questions)

1. **What is the current total power consumption of AI data centers globally in 2025, and how does this compare to 2023-2024 figures?**
   - Sub-questions: What percentage of total data center power is AI-specific? What is the growth rate?

2. **What are the power requirements for training frontier AI models (GPT-5 class, Gemini Ultra, Claude 4) compared to previous generations?**
   - Sub-questions: How much power does a single training run consume? What about inference at scale?

3. **What cooling technologies are being deployed in AI data centers in 2025, and what is their energy overhead?**
   - Sub-questions: Liquid cooling adoption rates? PUE (Power Usage Effectiveness) trends for AI-specific facilities?

4. **What is the current state of AI chip energy efficiency (performance per watt) for NVIDIA H100/H200/B100, AMD MI300X, Google TPU v5, and custom silicon?**
   - Sub-questions: How do efficiency improvements compare to compute demand growth?

5. **Where are new AI data centers being built in 2025, and what grid infrastructure challenges are emerging in those locations?**
   - Sub-questions: Grid connection wait times? Transmission bottlenecks? Local opposition?

### Economic/Business Implications (5 questions)

6. **What is the total capital investment in AI data center power infrastructure in 2025, and who are the major investors?**
   - Sub-questions: Cost per MW of AI-ready capacity? ROI expectations?

7. **How are electricity costs affecting AI development economics, and which companies have secured advantageous power agreements?**
   - Sub-questions: Power Purchase Agreements (PPAs) trends? Geographic arbitrage strategies?

8. **What is the business case for on-site power generation (nuclear, natural gas, renewables) at AI data centers?**
   - Sub-questions: Which companies are pursuing this? What are the regulatory hurdles?

9. **How is AI data center power demand affecting electricity prices for other industrial and residential consumers?**
   - Sub-questions: Documented price impacts? Grid priority disputes?

10. **What are the projected power costs as a percentage of total AI inference costs by 2027-2030?**
    - Sub-questions: Will power become the dominant cost factor? Impact on AI service pricing?

### Historical Context & Trends (3 questions)

11. **How has data center power consumption evolved from the pre-AI era (2015) through the generative AI boom (2022-2025)?**
    - Sub-questions: Key inflection points? Unexpected developments?

12. **What lessons from cryptocurrency mining's energy controversy are being applied (or ignored) in AI data center planning?**
    - Sub-questions: Policy responses? Public perception management?

13. **How have power efficiency improvements in traditional computing (Moore's Law era) compared to AI accelerator efficiency gains?**
    - Sub-questions: Is AI on a different trajectory? Why?

### Critical Perspectives & Controversies (4 questions)

14. **What are the environmental and carbon footprint claims made by major AI companies, and how do independent analyses assess their accuracy?**
    - Sub-questions: Greenwashing concerns? Scope 3 emissions accounting?

15. **What opposition movements or regulatory challenges have emerged against AI data center construction due to power concerns?**
    - Sub-questions: Specific cases? Outcomes? Community impacts?

16. **Is AI development sustainable from an energy perspective, and what are credible projections for power demand if current growth continues?**
    - Sub-questions: IEA projections? Academic studies? Industry rebuttals?

17. **What are the equity implications of AI data centers consuming power in regions with energy poverty or grid instability?**
    - Sub-questions: Documented cases? Corporate responses?

### Future Outlook & Solutions (3 questions)

18. **What emerging technologies (fusion, advanced nuclear, next-gen renewables) are being pursued specifically for AI data center power?**
    - Sub-questions: Timeline to deployment? Investment levels? Feasibility assessments?

19. **What policy and regulatory frameworks are being developed in 2025 to govern AI data center energy use?**
    - Sub-questions: EU regulations? US state-level actions? International coordination?

20. **What efficiency breakthroughs (algorithmic, hardware, operational) could significantly reduce AI power requirements?**
    - Sub-questions: Sparse models? Neuromorphic computing? Inference optimization?

## Research Methodology

### Primary Sources to Consult
- IEA (International Energy Agency) reports on data centers
- Company sustainability reports (Microsoft, Google, Amazon, Meta)
- Uptime Institute research
- EPRI (Electric Power Research Institute) studies
- Government agency reports (DOE, EPA, EU Commission)

### Secondary Sources
- Tech journalism (The Verge, Wired, Ars Technica, Data Center Dynamics)
- Financial analysis (Bloomberg, Reuters, industry analysts)
- Academic papers on data center efficiency
- Environmental organization reports (Greenpeace, Sierra Club)

### Data Validation Approach
- Cross-reference claims across 3+ sources for HIGH confidence
- Note discrepancies and industry vs. independent source disagreements
- Flag projections vs. measured data clearly
