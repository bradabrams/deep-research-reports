# Research Plan: Power Usage Trends in AI Data Centers 2025

## Topic Interpretation & Assumptions

### Scope Definition
This research examines the electricity consumption patterns, energy efficiency metrics, and power infrastructure demands of data centers specifically serving artificial intelligence workloads in 2025. The focus includes both training and inference operations for large language models (LLMs), generative AI, and other machine learning applications.

### Key Assumptions
1. **Temporal Focus**: Primary focus on 2024-2025 data with historical context from 2020-2024 for trend analysis
2. **Geographic Scope**: Global perspective with emphasis on major markets (US, EU, China, hyperscale regions)
3. **AI Definition**: Includes generative AI, LLMs, ML training/inference, GPU-accelerated computing workloads
4. **Data Center Types**: Covers hyperscale facilities, colocation, enterprise, and purpose-built AI training centers
5. **Power Metrics**: PUE (Power Usage Effectiveness), total MW capacity, energy consumption (TWh), carbon intensity
6. **Stakeholders**: Cloud providers (AWS, Azure, Google), AI companies (OpenAI, Anthropic, Meta), utilities, regulators, hardware vendors

### Research Boundaries
- **Included**: Direct power consumption, cooling energy, infrastructure overhead, grid impacts
- **Excluded**: Embodied carbon in hardware manufacturing, end-user device consumption, network transmission energy

## Research Questions

### Technical/Factual Core (Questions 1-6)

**1. What is the current total power consumption of AI-focused data centers globally in 2025?**
- Sub-questions: Regional breakdown, growth rate from 2024, comparison to traditional data centers

**2. How do power requirements differ between AI training and AI inference workloads?**
- Sub-questions: GPU vs TPU efficiency, batch processing vs real-time inference, model size impacts

**3. What is the average Power Usage Effectiveness (PUE) for AI data centers in 2025?**
- Sub-questions: Comparison to industry benchmarks, cooling technology impacts, climate zone variations

**4. How much power does training a single large language model consume?**
- Sub-questions: GPT-4 class models, open-source alternatives, efficiency improvements over time

**5. What hardware innovations are improving AI compute energy efficiency?**
- Sub-questions: NVIDIA H100/B100 vs predecessors, custom silicon (Google TPU, Amazon Trainium), liquid cooling adoption

**6. What is the projected power demand growth rate for AI data centers through 2030?**
- Sub-questions: Exponential vs linear projections, capacity constraints, stranded asset risks

### Economic/Business Implications (Questions 7-11)

**7. How are electricity costs impacting AI company operating expenses in 2025?**
- Sub-questions: Cost per inference, training budget allocations, geographic arbitrage strategies

**8. What capital investments are being made in power infrastructure for AI data centers?**
- Sub-questions: Grid interconnection costs, on-site generation, battery storage deployments

**9. How are utilities and grid operators responding to AI data center demand?**
- Sub-questions: Rate structures, demand response programs, infrastructure upgrades, queuing backlogs

**10. What is the economic impact of power constraints on AI development timelines?**
- Sub-questions: Delayed facility openings, compute allocation strategies, competitive implications

**11. How are power purchase agreements (PPAs) and renewable energy procurement evolving?**
- Sub-questions: 24/7 carbon-free energy commitments, additionality requirements, pricing trends

### Historical Context & Evolution (Questions 12-14)

**12. How has AI data center power consumption evolved since the emergence of modern LLMs (2020-2025)?**
- Sub-questions: ChatGPT launch impact, scaling law implications, efficiency gains vs demand growth

**13. What lessons from cryptocurrency mining apply to AI data center power management?**
- Sub-questions: Demand flexibility, grid stabilization services, regulatory responses

**14. How do current AI power trends compare to historical computing power transitions?**
- Sub-questions: Mainframe to PC era, cloud computing emergence, mobile computing growth

### Critical Perspectives & Controversies (Questions 15-20)

**15. What are the environmental sustainability concerns around AI data center power consumption?**
- Sub-questions: Carbon emissions trajectory, water usage for cooling, renewable energy sufficiency

**16. How accurate are industry projections for AI power demand, and what are the methodological debates?**
- Sub-questions: IEA vs Goldman Sachs estimates, assumption sensitivity, forecasting track records

**17. What regulatory pressures are emerging around AI data center energy use?**
- Sub-questions: EU efficiency mandates, US state-level policies, carbon pricing impacts

**18. Is there a tension between AI scaling ambitions and corporate sustainability commitments?**
- Sub-questions: Net-zero pledges, Scope 2/3 accounting, greenwashing concerns

**19. What are the grid reliability and energy security implications of concentrated AI power demand?**
- Sub-questions: Peak load management, transmission constraints, competing demand from EVs/electrification

**20. How equitable is the distribution of AI power consumption costs and benefits?**
- Sub-questions: Utility rate impacts on residential customers, community hosting burdens, job creation claims
